Why eight bytes work.

Before: direct immediates

MOV EAX,60 · MOV EDI,42 · SYSCALL

The two five-byte moves zero-extend their 32-bit values to RAX/RDI. SYSCALL contributes two bytes.

After: small immediates on the stack

PUSH 60 · POP RAX · PUSH 42 · POP RDI · SYSCALL

Each two-byte PUSH sign-extends an eight-bit immediate to a 64-bit word. A one-byte POP selects the register and restores RSP. These positive constants retain their intended values.

These mnemonics explain the handwritten bytes. Linux x86-64 CI runs the two curated ELF files and checks status 42; this page does not execute them in the browser.
